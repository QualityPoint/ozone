# Contact

Naqel is developed by **Quality Point**. Everything on the page that asks the visitor to act writes
to it: `naqelDeveloper` in `config/naqel.ts` holds its name and email (`erp@qp.sa`), and
`naqelContactHref` is the `mailto:` link.

| Where | What |
| --- | --- |
| Hero, "Get started" / "ابدأ الآن" | `mailto:erp@qp.sa` |
| Closing panel, "Email us" / "راسلنا بالبريد" | `mailto:erp@qp.sa` |
| Closing panel, "Developed by" / "تطوير" | Quality Point's logo on a white plate, its name as the alt text |

The email stays out of the messages (vue-i18n reserves `@`); the labels are
`naqel.hero.start`, `naqel.contact.email` and `naqel.contact.developedBy`.

## The logo

`src/assets/naqel/quality-point.webp` (360 px wide, lossless) is generated by
`scripts/build-brand-assets.py` (`write_qp_logo`) from Quality Point's own file,
`~/Documents/Companies-Media/QP/QP_Software/assests/QualityPoint_Logo_Software.png` (`QP_LOGO`): blue
on white, trimmed to its ink. It keeps its white ground, so the page shows it on a white plate in
both themes.
